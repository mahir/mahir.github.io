# No. 024 · the plan for a hundred cities

(Dev notes, not published.) The first twelve are drawn. These are the other 88. Each line is a
direction, not a specification: the drawing should be better than the note. Every face below is
checked against Google Fonts, including the script the title needs. The `google` query is
`family=<Name with + for spaces>` plus the weights you use (see the Google Fonts page).

Composition, so a hundred posters do not become forty skylines over water (the first twelve in
brackets):

- AERIAL: a bird's-eye or plan view [New York, Barcelona, Paris]
- SHORE: the city across water [Istanbul, Hong Kong, Venice, London, Cairo]
- STREET: at street level, facades close [Amsterdam, San Francisco]
- VISTA: city in a big landscape [Rio, Tokyo]
- HERO: one landmark, large, the poster built around it
- GRAPHIC: flat pattern or poster geometry first, the city inside it
- FRAME: seen through an arch, a door, a window or an ornamental frame
- FOLK: a naive or vernacular painting tradition
- NIGHT: after dark (no more than ten in all)
