Manhattan sundial

No. 003 by Mahir Yavuz. An abstract model of Manhattan, lit by the sun where it stands over New York right now and throwing the shadows the towers would throw. Drag through the day and the year to turn them. The street grid is set 29° east of true north, so on a few days a year the sun rises or sets straight down the cross streets: Manhattanhenge, in late May and mid-July at sunset, and in early December and January at sunrise.
