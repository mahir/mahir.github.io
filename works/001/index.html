About

Mahir Yavuz

Product and engineering leader, educator, working with data since 1998.

Connect on LinkedIn ↗

Mahir builds the teams and systems behind AI products and search, recommendation and ads platforms. Over two decades his teams have taken on data problems for companies including Nike, PwC, Google and Microsoft.

His work spans engineering, data visualization and media art, and has been shown at the Ars Electronica Festival, SIGGRAPH, ISEA and the Istanbul Biennial. He has taught since 2003, most recently at Columbia.
