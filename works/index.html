Mahir Yavuz

Product and engineering leader, educator and advisor, working with data since 1998.

Head of Engineering at AirOps. Before that, Mahir led engineering for Search, Recommendations and Ads at Etsy, co-founded Topos as its CTO, directed data science at R/GA and was a senior researcher at Ars Electronica Futurelab in Linz.

He has taught since 2003, at Columbia University, the University of Pennsylvania, Pratt and the University of Art and Design Linz, and his work has been shown at the Ars Electronica Festival, SIGGRAPH and the Istanbul Biennial.
